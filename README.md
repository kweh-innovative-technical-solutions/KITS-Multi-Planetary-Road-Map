## The Core Insight

The same monolithic 3D-printed habitat that protects a Krahn family 
in Liberia from wind, rain, and EMP — functions as a pressure vessel 
on the Moon and Mars.

Zero redesign. Zero air gap. Built from whatever aggregate exists 
at the deployment site. This is not science fiction. This is engineering.

## The Four Phases

### 🌍 Phase 0 — Liberia Pilot (Active Now)
**Location:** Kparblee District, Nimba County, Liberia, West Africa
**Goal:** 10–20 demonstration homes on Kweh family ancestral land

- Raw materials: Local mountain rock aggregate, iron ore, aluminum, titanium
- Energy: Off-grid solar photovoltaic + LiFePO4 battery storage
- Water: Rainwater harvesting and filtration
- Workforce: Young Engineers KITS graduates certified as 3D printer operators
- Governance: DAO framework installed from day one
- Financing: AfDB, SEFA, FAPA, USADF (applications in progress)

### 🍁 Phase 1 — Global Indigenous Scale
**Locations:** Treaty Nation territories (Alberta, Canada) + West Africa + Latin America

- Each community uses locally sourced aggregate where available
- KITS Liberia supply chain provides iron, aluminum, and titanium 
  for printer manufacturing at scale
- Liberty Coin Alliance connects all communities through shared 
  tokenized resource governance
- MudBots reseller partnership enables equipment access globally

### 🌕 Phase 2 — Lunar Base
**Target:** Lunar south polar region — water ice confirmed by NASA

- Printer systems adapted for lunar regolith aggregate printing
- ISRU (In-Situ Resource Utilization) — build with Moon materials
- Aerogel insulation for extreme thermal management (-173°C to +127°C)
- Solar + nuclear microreactor hybrid energy system
- Same DAO governance framework — community sovereignty from day one

### 🔴 Phase 3 — Mars Permanent Settlement

- Martian regolith aggregate printing — same printer, different aggregate
- AI-managed autonomous life support (open-source, community override)
- Closed-loop water, air, and food systems
- DAO constitution encoded into settlement from the first day of habitation
- The Krahn people's governance model becomes the template for 
  humanity's first permanent off-world civilization

### 🌌 Phase 4 — Interplanetary Standard

- KITS becomes the ISO standard for off-world habitation construction
- Open-source platform adopted by space agencies globally
- Indigenous communities on Earth own the intellectual foundation 
  of humanity's expansion into space

## Why Indigenous Communities Lead This

For thousands of years indigenous peoples have been told their land, 
their resources, and their knowledge belong to someone else.

This project reverses that permanently.

The Krahn people supply the raw earth. The earth builds the printers. 
The printers build the homes. The homes belong to the community — 
on this planet and every planet we reach.
